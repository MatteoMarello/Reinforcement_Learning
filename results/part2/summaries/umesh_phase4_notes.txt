Conclusion:
UDR gave the best transfer performance in Phase 4. It improved the source-to-target success rate from 0.50 to 1.00 and achieved performance close to the target-to-target upper bound.

ADR also improved over the source-to-target baseline, but it was weaker than UDR in this run. A possible explanation is that the adaptive randomization range did not expand quickly or effectively enough, while UDR exposed the policy to a wider fixed range of cube masses during training.
